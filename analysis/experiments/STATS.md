- free_bits = 0 in exp1 repeats archived (config, seed) fits; the two differ only by non-bitwise-reproducible training, so they are not independent replicates and are never pooled in the tests above.
- PCA32+Wilcoxon is deterministic, so its three seeds are identical; scVI and PCA are scored on training cells with a differential-expression ranking, not IG on test cells; their AUROC is against injected genes only.
- Recovery is defined per drug group; the MC-ContrastiveVI shared axis (where free bits act most directly) is excluded from `recovery_auroc` and reported in `recovery_auroc_shared_axis`.
- Probe accuracy is a different task for MC-ContrastiveVI (3-class condition, ungated drug block) than for contrastiveVI / multiGroupVI (within-study treated vs control); do not compare across methods.
- exp6 uses assumed treated test-cell counts (358 + 357) and iid Gaussian noise; the analytic dimension-split result does not depend on them, the spread does.
- Many tests are reported without family-wise correction except the Holm adjustment inside each section-4 table.

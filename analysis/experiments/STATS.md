## 5. Caveats: underpowered or not converged

- **WARNING: 20 of 54 exp1 fits did NOT converge (converged = False: they ran the full 300-epoch cap without early stopping firing). For those fits the free-bits dose-response is confounded with training budget; see section 1b.**
- **75 of 75 archived semi-synthetic fits did not converge (ran the full epoch cap; early stopping never fired).**
- **30 of 30 exp2 fits did not converge (ran the full epoch cap; early stopping never fired).**
- **7 of 12 exp7 fits did not converge (ran the full epoch cap; early stopping never fired).**
- exp2: 1 of 30 fits have an IG completeness error >= 0.01 even at 4,096 quadrature points.
- exp3 contrastiveVI / multiGroupVI and exp4 scVI train for a fixed number of epochs with no early stopping; convergence is not assessed.
- Section 1 pools n = 36 injected fits (6 per free_bits level, 3 seeds x 2 configurations) in one Spearman test; with 3 seeds per cell the per-configuration rows (n = 18) detect only |rho| of about 0.62 or more at 80% power. Seeds within a configuration share data, and the two configurations differ in effect size, so pooled P-values overstate independence.
- free_bits = 0 in exp1 repeats archived (config, seed) fits; the two differ only by non-bitwise-reproducible training, so they are not independent replicates and are never pooled in the tests above.
- PCA32+Wilcoxon is deterministic, so its three seeds are identical; scVI and PCA are scored on training cells with a differential-expression ranking, not IG on test cells; their AUROC is against injected genes only.
- Recovery is defined per drug group; the MC-ContrastiveVI shared axis (where free bits act most directly) is excluded from `recovery_auroc` and reported in `recovery_auroc_shared_axis`.
- Probe accuracy is a different task for MC-ContrastiveVI (3-class condition, ungated drug block) than for contrastiveVI / multiGroupVI (within-study treated vs control); do not compare across methods.
- exp6 uses assumed treated test-cell counts (358 + 357) and iid Gaussian noise; the analytic dimension-split result does not depend on them, the spread does.
- Many tests are reported without family-wise correction except the Holm adjustment inside each section-4 table.
